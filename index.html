Independent researcher · Utah

My background includes AI work at Progressive Insurance and iOS app development for Gift Connect. I now work full time as an independent interpretability researcher.

Based in Utah, I work with open model weights and measurement tools to study how neurons respond and how their roles change as models grow.

I share my research datasets, methods, and runnable code. I want the work to be useful to people asking their own questions about model internals, and easy to examine, challenge, and build on.
