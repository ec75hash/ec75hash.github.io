04Neuron responses2026

Similar weights.
Similar responses?

How alike are the responses of corresponding neurons 24 layers apart in Qwen3-32B?

I examined 288 neuron pairs on 10,000 fixed text windows. Corresponding partners often ranked above random alternatives, while absolute response resemblance was usually modest.

Cross-checkpoint Jacobian lens transfer

Generation & expert routing

Expert specialization during generation

Generation-time expert sets track the local subject of model outputs, revealing specialization that pooled prefill measurements can obscure. The study includes cross-checkpoint replication and controls for prompt design, position, and register.

I’m Jeffrey Shorthill, an independent mechanistic interpretability researcher investigating how computational structures and representations develop and change across language models.

I use structural analysis, activation measurements, and causal experiments to understand how models organize information, particularly around self-description and situational understanding.

I publish my methods, data, and findings openly to support independent verification, criticism, and collaboration.
